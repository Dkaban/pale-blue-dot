A note on interpretation

Objects launched, rocket launches, payloads, and satellites are separate measures. Countries can be assigned by operator, registry, launch site, or rocket provider; those methods are not interchangeable. Data coverage and update dates matter, so missing figures are shown as unavailable rather than invented.
